Firm Size and the Percolation Threshold of Stock-Return Correlation Networks: Evidence from Korea and Taiwan

Sungwoo Kang, Jong-Kook Kim

- Correlation-network percolation thresholds rise with firm size to the fourth quintile
- Korean thresholds average 0.524 in the smallest and 0.584 in the largest quintile
- In Korea and Taiwan, each rise to the next quintile up to the fourth is significant
- In Korea, adding stocks raises a network's threshold but hardly its mean correlation
- Under stated conditions, a model with no calibrated parameter predicts the rise
