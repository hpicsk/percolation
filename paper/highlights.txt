Firm Size and the Percolation Threshold of Stock-Return Correlation Networks: Evidence from Korea and Taiwan

Sungwoo Kang, Jong-Kook Kim

- Daily-return percolation thresholds rise with firm size to the fourth quintile
- Korean thresholds average 0.524 in the smallest and 0.584 in the largest quintile
- In Korea and Taiwan, thresholds rise significantly quintile by quintile to the fourth
- In Korea, mean correlation leaves 33% of the size gap in thresholds unexplained
- Under stated conditions, an uncalibrated model predicts the rise with firm size
